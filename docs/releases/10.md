# MBooru 10

Android version code: 1383. Existing package, signing identity and library records are retained.

- Bottom navigation is removed, without a reserved bar-height gap.
- The primary search field has direct Favorites and Settings buttons. Its three-dot menu is removed.
- Favorites is the single library destination for previously saved artwork, local likes and collections. Existing records are read in place; no destructive migration or export/import is required.
- Site switching remains in the side panel, available by edge swipe or Settings → Side panel. Sites management remains in Settings.
- The old bottom search panel is removed from all secondary browsing screens. Their search, suggestions, filters and supported actions appear at the top, with direct buttons instead of an overflow menu.
